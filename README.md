Files cited by ByteDance-Seed/EdgeBench issues and PRs for arc_compiler_runtime, schemathesis_datagen_pipeline, juliet_vulnerability_analyzer, integer_compression_codec and wesnoth_tactical_ai.

Environment for every grade: an E2B sandbox (template `harbor-worker-v4`), 8 vCPU, 7964 MiB RAM, Docker 29.4.1, Python 3.12 via `uv`, community `f92857a3`, `tools/judge_local.py` at the task's limits. Each folder's `env.txt` is that worker's record; the OS release wasn't recorded.

- `arc_compiler_runtime/`: `agent36.tar.gz` (an Opus 5.5 submission from our own run), the long write-up, `rerun/` (the latest two-pass grade on the published judge image: summary, image digests, grade JSONs without the log tail, environment), and `c24_public_arc_datagen.py` with its output (counts over public reports of other solvers; the reports themselves are in the Hugging Face dataset `adafadaq/edgebench-opus-5-5-xhigh-rollouts` and are not copied here).
- `schemathesis_datagen_pipeline/`: `agent2.tar.gz` (Opus 5.5, our own run), `mk.py` (builds `agent2_named` and `agent2_full`), `names.py` (the alias layer), `ref415.tar.gz` (the empty starter plus `schemathesis/specs/openapi/headers.py` from the PyPI wheel of Schemathesis 4.15.0, sha256 `b5f734424a4b3558fc71a84b337a7773f99707e6f13864c1ebb26e5abb360e89`, MIT licence), the long write-up, and `grades/` (every old and new grade of the PR's table, image keys, environment).
- `juliet_vulnerability_analyzer/`: `agent8.tar.gz` and `agent8_off.tar.gz` (Opus 5.5, our own run), `grades/` (old and new grade JSONs with their log tails, image tags, environment), the long write-up, and the label-by-statement comparison script with its counts-only output.
- `integer_compression_codec/`: the long write-up and the scripts behind the mapping and pair counts, with saved output.
- `wesnoth_tactical_ai/`: the constructed archives, job scripts and raw outputs, `diagnose.sh`, and the results scripts. The public Qwen 3.8 archive is cited by path and sha256 in the PR, not copied.
